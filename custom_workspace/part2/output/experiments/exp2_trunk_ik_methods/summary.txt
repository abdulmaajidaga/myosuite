Experiment 2: Why IK-Driven Trunk Failed
==================================================

Trunk IK peak vs CS plate ground truth — Pearson r:
  IK-free (no damping)                r=-0.689  p=0.1303  n=6
  IK-low (TR=0.05)                    r=-0.864  p=0.0263  n=6
  IK-heavy (TR=50)                    r=-0.874  p=0.0227  n=6
  SVD prescribed (best)               r=1.000  p=0.0000  n=6

Conclusion: IK-free, IK-low, IK-heavy all show low/noisy correlation
with CS plate ground truth. SVD prescribed trivially matches (r≈1).
This confirms IK cannot reliably determine trunk angle from arm markers.
